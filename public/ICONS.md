# Icons

`favicon.svg`, `favicon-32.png` and `apple-touch-icon.png` are the airplane (U+2708) from
[OpenMoji](https://openmoji.org), the open-source emoji and icon project, licensed
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Changes: placed on a green rounded square and scaled up so it reads at browser-tab size; the PNGs are
rasterised from `favicon.svg`. These icon files stay under CC BY-SA 4.0 (the license applies to them only,
not to the rest of this repository).
